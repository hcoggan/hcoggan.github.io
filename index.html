I am a postdoc in the MIT linguistics department working with Prof. Alan Chen. My research concerns the syntax and semantics of evidentiality across typologically diverse languages, with a focus on how speakers encode the source and reliability of information in grammar.

Before MIT, I completed my PhD at the University of Chicago (2023), where my dissertation examined evidential systems in three Tibeto-Burman languages. I will be on the job market in fall 2025.

Research Interests

Evidentiality · Epistemic modality · Fieldwork (Tibeto-Burman) · Semantics–pragmatics interface · Cross-linguistic typology
