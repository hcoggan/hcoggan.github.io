I am a research scientist in the Cava Lab at Boston Children's Hospital. My work focuses on algorithmic fairness in clinical machine learning — I conduct epidemiological analyses of large-scale electronic health record databases to study how demographic factors shape clinician decision-making in the emergency department, and develop real-time fairness monitoring tools for ML systems deployed in clinical settings.

I completed my PhD in computational biology at University College London (2025), supervised by Prof. Jasmin Fisher and in collaboration with the McGranahan and Swanton laboratories. My dissertation modelled the role of cell plasticity in early-stage lung cancer using agent-based simulations and Bayesian inference on data from the TRACERx patient cohort. Before that I read Natural Sciences (Physics) at the University of Cambridge, graduating with a high first.
