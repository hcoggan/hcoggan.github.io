I am a postdoc in the Computational Health Informatics Program at Boston Children's Hospital, working with William La Cava. My research concerns the development of algorithms for real-time clinical monitoring in the emergency department. I also study how healthcare disparities arise in clinical decision-making process, both in the emergency department and on the labor ward.

Before moving to Boston, I completed my PhD at University College London (2025). My doctoral thesis focused on large-scale agent-based simulations of lung tumour evolution, and was supervised by Jasmin Fisher, with support from Nicholas McGranahan and Karen M. Page.
